Tool 6 · Development plan

What would make you more credible for the work you want next?

Do not collect training for the sake of collecting training. Identify the capability, then choose the smallest useful way to build evidence.

AI is a career fundamental now—but use it like a professional.

Use AI to compare job descriptions, identify recurring skill themes, translate accomplishments into transferable language, rehearse interviews, or brainstorm adjacent roles. Do not use it to invent experience you do not have.
